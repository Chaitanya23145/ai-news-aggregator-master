Frequently Asked Questions.

Everything you need to know about our daily AI briefs, 1-click unfollow, and privacy standards.

How do I unsubscribe or stop receiving emails?

You can unfollow/unsubscribe at any time in 1 click. Either click the link in the footer below or click the 1-click unsubscribe link at the bottom of every briefing email. Your email is immediately marked inactive in MongoDB Atlas, dispatches permanently stop, and our founder receives an instant audit alert.

Is Synapse AI really 100% free?

Yes. There are no credit cards, hidden subscriptions, or paywalls. Synapse AI runs autonomously and is supported by voluntary community tips through our button.

What happens to my data after unsubscribing?

Your email is preserved strictly in an inactive audit state in our database to ensure our dispatch workers never accidentally contact you again. We never sell, rent, or share your contact info with third parties or advertisers.

Can I re-subscribe if I change my mind later?

Yes! Simply enter your email address into any subscription box on this website. Our database will detect your address and reactivate your daily briefings starting the very next morning at 8:00 AM.

What primary sources does the pipeline monitor?

Autonomous scrapers monitor arXiv cs.AI preprints, official research releases from OpenAI, Anthropic, Hugging Face, TechCrunch AI, and verified YouTube research seminars.

What time does the daily brief arrive?

Every morning at 8:00 AM, formatted in a high-contrast dark mode briefing designed to be read in exactly 2 minutes before your morning engineering sync.
